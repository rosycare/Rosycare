      Care we bring to every area

      Whichever office serves your town, the same Rosy team provides the full range of home health care at home. The six services below are the standard home health list, shown as an example — the live page carries only the services Rosy confirms it provides.

      Skilled nursing

      Wound care, medication support, post-surgery care and health monitoring by licensed nurses.

      Physical therapy

      Strength, balance and mobility work to get you moving safely again after illness or surgery.

      Occupational therapy

      Practical help relearning everyday tasks — dressing, cooking, bathing — with confidence.

      Speech therapy

      Support with speech, language and swallowing after a stroke or other condition.

      Medical social services

      Guidance on community resources, benefits and planning, for patients and their families.

      Home health aide

      Hands-on help with personal care and daily routines, under the direction of your care team.
